Motivation

2025/01/02 · 240 words · 2 minutes to read

Why write this blog? 🔗

There’s 2 things that make me feel complete: helping people and building things.

And that’s the short answer to why I decided to blog: I can document the things I build, and hopefully the posts will help people.

Helping Others 🔗

I recently found myself between startups without any major financial pressure. So before starting anything new, I did a ton of work learning about myself. What I mean is all the big questions we all have but sometimes avoid: “who am I?”, “why do I feel?”, “what makes me happy?”.

It was answering that last question that really motivated me to start writing. The days where I was able to help someone, or build something new where the days I went to sleep happy and fulfilled. This is a simple realization in words, but transformed how I approach life.

Building Things 🔗

A mentor of mine once said “It’s one thing to do good work, but if you don’t tell anyone, then no one will know”. This was early in my career and has always been relevant (despite me sometimes forgetting), and I think that I can also apply this outside my career.

I really do build some cool things: Python tools, electronics, home automation, home renovations; I think I should have a place to tell people about those things too!

So here I am. Hopefully I can build neat things and help someone with their project.
